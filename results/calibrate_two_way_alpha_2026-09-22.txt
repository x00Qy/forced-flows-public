  WHY THIS DIFFERS FROM THE PHASE 2 PRE-CHECK'S 42.44 bps.
  That figure clusters on the DATE only (G = 1,203 entry dates,
  m = 1.48, rho = 0.2), which treats the 1,781 entries as ~1,203
  near-independent draws. Clustering on the STOCK as well does not:
  1,781 entries sit in 141 stocks, one holding 75, so
  sum_s c_s^2 = 53,887 against n = 1,781. That ratio -- not the
  threshold calibration -- is what moves the MDE. Moving from the
  nominal 0.01 to alpha* costs 6.5% of the MDE; adding the stock
  dimension costs 3.0x.
